Delete your Trukbite account

You can delete your account and the data tied to it at any time, right in the app. If you no longer have the app, email us and we'll do it for you.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Trukbite and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It happens right away and can't be undone.

By email

Email support@trukbite.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the phone number you sign in with. To make sure the request is really yours, we'll confirm with you before deleting. We delete the account within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and the phone number you sign in with
  • Your name, the trucks, parks and events you follow, your alert settings and the alerts in your Alerts tab
  • Your reviews, review photos and dish ratings
  • Your saved location, your phone's notification address, and the people you blocked
  • If you're the only owner of a truck: the truck too, with its menu, photos, schedule and permit photo

What we keep

  • Truck activity totals (like "40 people viewed this truck this week") stay, so truck owners keep their stats. Your account's link to them is removed, so they can't be traced back to you.
  • Catering requests you sent stay with the truck you sent them to, since they're part of that truck's records. Feedback you sent us, and parks or events you suggested, also stay. In each case, the link to your account is removed.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, only for as long as it requires.

Questions? Email support@trukbite.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
