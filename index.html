Getting on Trukbite

  1. Sign in with your phone number. No passwords, just a text code.
  2. Add the basics. Your truck's name, cuisine and logo, plus a photo of your permit (only our team sees it).
  3. We approve you, usually within a day.
  4. Tap Open here the next time you park.

FAQ

Questions

Is Trukbite free?

Yes. Trukbite is always free for customers. Truck owners can list for free too, and Pro ($14.99/month) adds extras like stats, specials and staff accounts.

When does it launch?

We're signing up our founding trucks now, so the map is full on day one. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know the moment it's live.

Will it be on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both.

Do trucks have to share their location all day?

No. A truck taps "Open here" when it opens, and it closes on its own at closing time. Nothing is tracked in between.

Is it only in San Antonio?

We're starting in San Antonio and getting it right here first. Austin, Dallas and Houston are next.

I run a truck. How do I get on?

Join the waitlist and choose "food truck owner". The first 20 trucks are founding trucks: Pro free for 6 months after launch, then 50% off for life.
